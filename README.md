# Streak downloads

Streak is a daily todo app built around streaks. This page only hosts the installers.

## Download

| Device | Download |
| --- | --- |
| **Windows** (10 or 11) | [Streak-Setup.exe](https://github.com/thesanjayoff/streak-downloads/releases/latest/download/Streak-Setup.exe) |
| **Android** (7.0 or newer) | [Streak.apk](https://github.com/thesanjayoff/streak-downloads/releases/latest/download/Streak.apk) |

These links always give the newest version. Older versions and what changed are on the [Releases](../../releases) page.
There is no iPhone or Mac version.

## Install

**Windows:** run `Streak-Setup.exe`. It installs for your user only and needs no admin rights. The installer is not
code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC": click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.

**Android:** download `Streak.apk` on your phone and open it. Android will ask you to allow installs from the app you
opened it with (your browser or Files app): allow it for this once. If Google Play Protect warns about an unknown app,
choose *Install anyway*. To update, download the newer file and install it over the old one. Do not uninstall first.

## Signing in

You need an account before you can sign in, and there is no sign-up inside the app. The person who shared Streak with you
creates it for your email address. After that you can sign in with your email and password, or tap **Continue with Google**
if your Google account uses that same email address.

**The person who runs Streak can see your tasks and streaks, and can add tasks to your list.** They cannot change or delete the tasks you make. The app tells you so on the sign-in screen. If you
would rather keep your tasks private, tap *Not now: use this device only* and Streak works fully on its own, with no account.

## Notes

- The app works offline and keeps a full copy of your data on your device. With an account, it also syncs to your other devices.
- Each release lists the files for that version, with their SHA-256 checksums. Only install files that come from this page.
